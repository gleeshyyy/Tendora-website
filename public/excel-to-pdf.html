Excel to PDF Converter

Convert Excel workbooks into a PDF. Every worksheet with data is included, and processing happens in your browser. Your workbook is not uploaded to Tendora.

Select an Excel workbook

Supported: XLS, XLSX, XLSM, XLSB

All populated worksheets are included in workbook order. Wide or very large sheets may be scaled down or split across multiple pages. Excel charts, macros, formulas' live calculations, and complex formatting may not be reproduced exactly; displayed cell values are exported. Review the PDF before using it.
