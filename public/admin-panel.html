Administrator accounts

Only the Owner can create accounts, assign roles, disable access and reset passwords.

Role permissions

Owner: Full access, including administrator account management.

Admin: Manage vacancies, recruitment sources, discovery, Recycle Bin and Activity Logs. Cannot manage administrator accounts.

Editor: Create and edit vacancy drafts and process discovery candidates. Cannot publish or reject vacancies.

Reviewer: Review, publish or reject vacancy drafts and review discovery candidates. Cannot create or edit vacancies.
