Word/Excel to PDF Converter

Convert Word documents and Excel workbooks into PDF using one tool. Processing happens in your browser; your files are not uploaded to Tendora.

Select a Word document or Excel workbook

Supported: DOC, DOCX, TXT, XLS, XLSX, XLSM, XLSB

Word: DOCX formatting is retained where supported. Legacy DOC files are extracted as text, so original page layout and rich formatting may not be preserved. Excel: all populated worksheets are included in workbook order; charts, macros, formulas and complex formatting may not be reproduced exactly. Wide or very large sheets may be scaled down or split across pages. Review the PDF before using it.
