PDF to Word

Turn a text-based PDF into an editable Microsoft Word document (.docx). Your PDF is processed locally in this browser and is not uploaded to Tendora.

Select a PDF file

Important: This tool extracts selectable text and rebuilds it as editable Word paragraphs. It does not reproduce the original PDF layout, columns, tables, images, signatures or exact fonts. Scanned/image-only PDFs need OCR first; they may produce an empty document here. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.
