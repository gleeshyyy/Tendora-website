Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Operated by GLEESHYYY

This Privacy Policy explains how Tendora (“Tendora”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you visit tendora.gleesh.workers.dev, browse job listings or use our document tools.

Important: Tendora currently lists jobs in Rwanda and provides browser-based document tools worldwide. Job applications are generally handled by the employer or an external application destination, not submitted to Tendora.

1. Who operates Tendora

Tendora is operated by GLEESHYYY. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact quaviouselyse@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Basic website and usage information: pages visited, document tool usage events, and a randomly generated browser identifier. The identifier is stored in your browser's local storage. Our analytics service receives it and stores a derived hash with page paths and event times. We use this to understand website usage and improve the service.
  • Preference information: language and light/dark theme choices are stored in preference cookies. These cookies are used to remember your settings. The cover-letter builder may also save the draft details and signature you enter in that tool in your browser's local storage so you can continue later on the same browser.
  • Job application click information: when you click an application link, we may record the vacancy reference and click event for basic vacancy analytics. The employer or destination website may separately collect information you provide there.
  • Administrator information: Tendora's restricted administration system processes administrator email addresses, display names, password hashes and salts, profile photos, login/session records and administrative audit events. This information is used to secure and operate the website.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we receive the email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to include.

Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request. Tendora does not currently provide candidate accounts or a platform form for uploading job applications.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate and secure Tendora; remember your language and theme preferences; understand visits and use of tools; measure application-link clicks; manage and verify job listings; maintain administrator access and audit activity; respond to enquiries; prevent abuse; and meet legal obligations.

4. Document tools and files

Our document tools are designed to process selected files in your browser on your device. Tendora's tool pages state when processing takes place locally. We do not receive the contents of a file merely because you select it in a browser-based tool. Some tools download software libraries or processing engines from third-party content delivery networks when needed. Your browser may therefore connect to those providers to load the tool. Check the notice on the individual tool page, and avoid using a tool with confidential files on a device you do not control.

5. Job listings and external applications

We publish job information researched from employers and other public sources. When you choose to apply, you may be sent to an employer's website or another external destination, or your email application may open. That destination is controlled by the relevant third party and has its own privacy practices. Review its privacy policy before providing personal information. Tendora does not control what an employer collects or how it uses an application.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We use preference cookies named tendora_theme and tendora_language to remember your display and language choices. They are set for up to one year. The website also uses browser local storage for a randomly generated analytics identifier, and the cover-letter builder uses local storage for your saved in-browser draft. You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings; doing so may reset preferences or remove a locally saved draft. Blocking storage may affect some features.

7. Service providers and international processing

We rely on service providers to host and deliver the website, store operational data and deliver website assets. These providers may process technical or personal information in countries other than Rwanda. Some document-tool libraries are delivered by third-party content delivery networks. We do not sell personal information.

Location and legal notice: Tendora's current infrastructure includes Cloudflare services. The live database service has reported a European serving region. This means cross-border storage or processing may be involved. Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 regulates international transfers and storage outside Rwanda. We are reviewing the applicable registration, authorization and safeguards. This policy is not a statement that all required regulatory approvals have already been obtained.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including security, administration, analytics and legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type. Locally saved drafts remain in your browser until you delete them or clear browser storage. Email correspondence may remain in our mailbox for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related follow-up.

9. Security

We use administrative access controls, password hashing, session controls and technical safeguards intended to protect information. No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a secure device and do not share administrator credentials.

10. Your privacy rights

Under Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 and other applicable law, your rights may include requesting information about processing and a copy of your personal data, correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, erasure where legal conditions are met, restriction of processing, objection, portability where applicable, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. You may also complain to Rwanda's Data Protection and Privacy Office. To make a request, email quaviouselyse@gmail.com and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

11. Children's privacy

Tendora is a general job and document service and is not designed for children. Where we know that personal data relates to a child under sixteen (16), applicable Rwandan law may require consent from a person holding parental responsibility. Do not knowingly submit another person's personal information without authority to do so.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Tendora's features, providers or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use after an update means you have had the opportunity to read the revised policy; it does not replace consent where consent is legally required.

Politiki y’Ibanga n’Imikoreshereze y’Amakuru bwite

Yavuguruwe: 21 Ugushyingo 2026 · Ikorwa na GLEESHYYY

Iyi Politiki isobanura uko Tendora (“Tendora”, “twe” cyangwa “yacu”) ikusanya, ikoresha, ibika kandi isangiza amakuru igihe usuye tendora.gleesh.workers.dev, ureba amatangazo y’akazi cyangwa ukoresha ibikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko.

Icyitonderwa: Tendora itangaza akazi ko mu Rwanda kandi itanga ibikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko bikorera muri browser yawe ku isi hose. Gusaba akazi muri rusange bikorerwa ku rubuga rw’umukoresha cyangwa ahandi hantu hagenewe kwakira ubusabe, ntibikorerwa muri Tendora.

1. Ukoresha Tendora

Tendora ikorwa na GLEESHYYY. Ku bibazo cyangwa ubusabe bujyanye n’ibanga ry’amakuru, twandikire kuri quaviouselyse@gmail.com.

2. Amakuru dukusanya

  • Amakuru y’imikoreshereze y’urubuga: amapaji wasuye, igihe ibikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko bikoreshejwe, n’indangamuntu ya browser ikorwa mu buryo bw’impanuka. Iyi ndangamuntu ibikwa muri local storage ya browser yawe. Sisitemu y’isesengura yakira iyi ndangamuntu ikayihindura hash, ikabika iyo hash hamwe n’amapaji wasuye n’igihe ibyabereyeho. Ibi bidufasha kumenya uko urubuga rukoreshwa no kurunoza.
  • Amakuru y’ibyo ukunda: guhitamo ururimi n’insanganyamatsiko y’amabara (urumuri cyangwa umwijima) bibikwa muri cookies kugira ngo twibuke ibyo wahisemo. Igikoresho cyo kwandika ibaruwa isaba akazi gishobora kubika inyandiko n’umukono winjijemo muri local storage ya browser yawe, kugira ngo ukomeze kuyikoresha kuri iyo browser.
  • Amakuru ajyanye no gukanda ku murongo wo gusaba akazi: iyo ukanze umurongo wo gusaba akazi, dushobora kubika nimero iranga iryo tangazo n’igikorwa cyo gukanda hagamijwe gusesengura imikoreshereze. Urubuga rw’umukoresha cyangwa urundi rubuga rugana rushobora na rwo gukusanya amakuru utanga.
  • Amakuru y’abayobozi ba Tendora: sisitemu yihariye y’ubuyobozi ibika aderesi za email, amazina agaragara, amakuru ahinduwe y’ibanga (password hashes na salts), amafoto y’imyirondoro, amakuru yo kwinjira no gusohoka, n’ibikorwa by’ubuyobozi. Akoreshwa mu gucunga no kurinda urubuga.
  • Ubutumwa utwoherereza: iyo utwoherereje email, tubona aderesi yawe, ubutumwa n’andi makuru cyangwa amadosiye wahisemo kohereza.

Irinde kohereza amakuru y’ibanga rikomeye keretse bibaye ngombwa. Kugeza ubu Tendora nta konti z’abashaka akazi ifite kandi nta fomu yo koherezamo dosiye zo gusaba akazi itanga.

3. Impamvu dukoresha amakuru

Dukoresha amakuru mu gutuma Tendora ikora neza kandi itekana; kwibuka ururimi n’amabara wahisemo; gusobanukirwa uko urubuga n’ibikoresho byarwo bikoreshwa; kubara abakanze imirongo yo gusaba akazi; gucunga no kugenzura amatangazo y’akazi; kurinda konti z’abayobozi no kubika amateka y’ibikorwa; gusubiza ibibazo; gukumira ikoreshwa nabi; no kubahiriza amategeko.

4. Ibikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko n’amadosiye

Ibikoresho byacu byagenewe gutunganyiriza amadosiye wahisemo muri browser yawe, ku gikoresho cyawe. Amapaji y’ibikoresho asobanura igihe igikorwa gikorerwa muri browser. Kuba wahisemo idosiye muri browser ntibihita bivuga ko Tendora yakiriye ibiyirimo. Bimwe mu bikoresho bishobora gukura porogaramu nto cyangwa moteri yo gutunganya amadosiye kuri serivisi z’abandi zitanga amadosiye kuri internet. Bityo browser yawe ishobora guhura n’izo serivisi kugira ngo igikoresho gikore. Soma ubutumwa buri ku rupapuro rw’igikoresho kandi wirinde gukoresha amadosiye y’ibanga ku gikoresho utagenzura.

5. Amatangazo y’akazi no gusaba akazi ahandi

Dushyira ahagaragara amakuru y’akazi dukura ku bakoresha n’ahandi hantu hatanga amakuru ku mugaragaro. Iyo uhisemo gusaba akazi, ushobora koherezwa ku rubuga rw’umukoresha cyangwa ahandi hantu, cyangwa email yawe igafunguka. Aho hantu hacungwa n’undi muntu kandi hafite politiki yabwo bwite. Soma politiki yabwo mbere yo gutanga amakuru yawe. Tendora ntigenzura amakuru umukoresha akusanya cyangwa uko ayakoresha.

6. Cookies n’ububiko bwa browser

Dukoresha cookies zitwa tendora_theme na tendora_language kugira ngo twibuke amabara n’ururimi wahisemo. Zishobora kumara umwaka umwe. Urubuga kandi rukoresha local storage ya browser mu kubika indangamuntu y’isesengura ikorwa mu buryo bw’impanuka; igikoresho cy’ibaruwa yo gusaba akazi na cyo gikoresha local storage mu kubika inyandiko yawe. Ushobora gusiba cookies na local storage ukoresheje igenamiterere rya browser. Ibyo bishobora gusubiza inyuma ibyo wahisemo cyangwa bigasiba inyandiko wari warabitse. Guhagarika ububiko bishobora gutuma bimwe mu bikoresho bidakora neza.

7. Abatanga serivisi n’itunganywa ry’amakuru mu mahanga

Dukoresha abandi badufasha kwakira no gutanga urubuga, kubika amakuru akenewe mu mikorere yarwo no gutanga amadosiye y’urubuga. Abo batanga serivisi bashobora gutunganya amakuru ya tekiniki cyangwa amakuru bwite mu bihugu bitari u Rwanda. Bimwe mu bikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko bikoresha amadosiye atangwa n’izindi serivisi zo kuri internet. Ntiducuruza amakuru bwite.

Icyitonderwa ku bubiko n’amategeko: Ibikorwaremezo bya Tendora birimo serivisi za Cloudflare. Serivisi ya database iriho ubu yagaragaje ko ikorera mu karere k’u Burayi. Bityo, bishobora kuba birimo kubika cyangwa gutunganya amakuru hanze y’u Rwanda. Itegeko ry’u Rwanda No 058/2021 rigena uburyo bwo kohereza no kubika amakuru hanze y’u Rwanda. Turimo gusuzuma iyandikwa, impushya n’ingamba bisabwa. Iyi politiki ntivuga ko impushya zose zisabwa zimaze kuboneka.

8. Igihe tubika amakuru

Tubika amakuru igihe gikenewe mu buryo bushyize mu gaciro kugira ngo tugere ku mpamvu zasobanuwe muri iyi politiki, harimo umutekano, imiyoborere, isesengura n’inshingano z’amategeko. Igihe cyo kubika gishobora gutandukana bitewe n’ubwoko bw’amakuru. Inyandiko zabitswe muri browser zigumaho kugeza uzisibye cyangwa ugasiba ububiko bwa browser. Ubutumwa bwo kuri email bushobora kugumaho igihe gikenewe kugira ngo dusubize ikibazo n’ibindi bijyanye na cyo.

9. Umutekano

Dukoresha uburyo bwo kugenzura uburenganzira bw’abayobozi, kubika amagambo y’ibanga mu buryo buhishwe, gucunga igihe konti imara ifunguye n’izindi ngamba za tekiniki zigamije kurinda amakuru. Nta rubuga cyangwa uburyo bwo kohereza no kubika amakuru bushobora kwizezwa umutekano usesuye. Koresha igikoresho gitekanye kandi ntusangize abandi amakuru yo kwinjira kuri konti y’ubuyobozi.

10. Uburenganzira bwawe ku makuru bwite

Ukurikije Itegeko ry’u Rwanda No 058/2021 n’andi mategeko akurikizwa, uburenganzira bwawe bushobora kuba bukubiyemo gusaba amakuru ku buryo amakuru yawe akoreshwa no guhabwa kopi yayo, gukosora amakuru atari yo cyangwa atuzuye, gusaba kuyasiba igihe ibisabwa n’amategeko byujujwe, kugabanya imikoreshereze yayo, kuyitambamira, kuyimurira ahandi aho bishoboka no gukuraho uburenganzira watanze igihe ari bwo bushingirwaho. Ubu burenganzira bugengwa n’ibisabwa n’amategeko n’ibidasanzwe ateganya. Ushobora kandi kugeza ikirego ku Biro bishinzwe Kurinda Amakuru Bwite mu Rwanda. Kugira ngo usabe serivisi ijyanye n’uburenganzira bwawe, andikira quaviouselyse@gmail.com usobanure icyo usaba. Dushobora kugusaba kwemeza umwirondoro wawe. Amwe mu makuru ashobora kugumana igihe amategeko abyemera cyangwa abitegeka.

11. Amakuru y’abana

Tendora ni urubuga rusange rw’akazi n’ibikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko; ntirugenewe abana. Iyo tuzi ko amakuru bwite yerekeye umwana utarageza ku myaka 16, amategeko akurikizwa mu Rwanda ashobora gusaba ko haboneka uburenganzira bw’umuntu ufite inshingano zo kumurera. Ntugatange amakuru bwite y’undi muntu utabifitiye uburenganzira.

12. Impinduka kuri iyi politiki

Dushobora kuvugurura iyi politiki igihe serivisi za Tendora, abayifasha cyangwa ibisabwa n’amategeko bihindutse. Inyandiko nshya izashyirwa kuri uru rupapuro hamwe n’itariki yo kuyivugurura. Gukomeza gukoresha urubuga nyuma y’impinduka biguha amahirwe yo gusoma politiki nshya; ntibisimbura uburenganzira cyangwa ubwumvikane busabwa n’amategeko.
