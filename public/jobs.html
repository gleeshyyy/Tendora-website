Finding jobs in Rwanda with Tendora

Tendora focuses on job opportunities in Rwanda. Vacancies are researched and reviewed before publication, and listings may be updated or removed when an employer changes or closes a recruitment process.

Open a vacancy to review the employer, role, location, qualifications, application instructions and deadline. Applications currently take place through the employer's official recruitment channel, not through a Tendora account.

Stay safe when applying for work

  • Confirm the vacancy and deadline on the employer's official website.
  • Never pay a recruiter to apply, attend an interview or receive a job offer.
  • Be careful with requests for passwords, mobile money PINs or sensitive financial information.

Tendora aims to provide useful vacancy information, but applicants should always confirm current details directly with the employer.
