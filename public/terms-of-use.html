1. Abemerewe gukoresha urubuga n’imikoreshereze yemewe

Ushobora gukoresha Tendora gusa mu buryo bwemewe n’amategeko kandi ukurikiza aya Mabwiriza. Ni wowe ufite inshingano zo kureba ko imikoreshereze yawe yubahiriza amategeko akureba. Serivisi igenewe abantu bashaka amakuru y’akazi n’abakeneye ibikoresho byo gutunganya inyandiko.

2. Amatangazo y’akazi n’ukuri kw’amakuru

Dukora ubushakashatsi kandi tugenzura intoki amakuru y’akazi mbere yo kuyashyira ku rubuga igihe bishoboka. Ariko imyanya y’akazi ishobora guhinduka, gufungwa mbere y’igihe, gukurwaho cyangwa kuba irimo amakosa nyuma yo kuyigenzura. Ntabwo twizeza ko umwanya w’akazi ugifunguye, ko amakuru yose yuzuye cyangwa ari ukuri, cyangwa ko umukoresha azagusubiza. Banza urebe itangazo ry’umukoresha ku rubuga rwe kandi wemeze amakuru y’ingenzi mbere yo gusaba akazi.

Aho bishoboka, Tendora itanga umurongo ujya ku itangazo ry’umwimerere cyangwa aho amakuru yaturutse. Gutanga uwo murongo ntibivuga ko Tendora ishyigikiye umukoresha, umwanya w’akazi cyangwa urundi rubuga.

3. Gusaba akazi no gushaka abakozi

  • Ubusabe bw’akazi busanzwe bwoherezwa ku mukoresha cyangwa kuri serivisi yo hanze. Kugeza ubu Tendora ntirakira cyangwa ngo ibike CV z’abashaka akazi, impapuro zo gusaba akazi cyangwa izindi nyandiko binyuze muri fomu yo gusaba akazi.
  • Iyo ukanze umurongo wo gusaba akazi, uba uvuye kuri Tendora cyangwa ugafungura email. Amabwiriza na politiki by’aho woherejwe ni byo bigenga amakuru utanga.
  • Tendora ntifata ibyemezo byo guha abantu akazi, ntizeza ko uzahabwa ikiganiro cyangwa akazi, ntigenzura buri mukoresha mu buryo bwigenga kandi ntihagararira usaba akazi cyangwa umukoresha.
