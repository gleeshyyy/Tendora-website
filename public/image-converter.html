Convert an image to another format

Select an image to identify its source format, then choose an available output format. Tendora supports common browser-readable images, including JPG, PNG and WebP.

Different formats suit different uses. JPG is commonly used for photographs, PNG supports transparency and lossless images, and WebP can provide compact web images.

Before converting

  • Keep the original file if you may need its full quality or transparency.
  • Choose PNG when transparent backgrounds matter.
  • Review the converted image before sharing or publishing it.
